# TELECOM CUSTOMER CHURN ANALYSIS IN DATA ANALYTICS USING POWER BI 

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Behavioural Drift and Early Churn Analysis System** focuses on analyzing customer behaviour, identifying churn patterns, and predicting customers who are likely to leave a telecommunications service.

The project combines Data Analytics, Power BI, and Machine Learning to gain insights into customer churn and support data-driven customer retention strategies.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics, tenure, contract types, payment methods, and service usage.
- Identify key factors associated with customer churn.
- Build machine learning models to predict potential customer churn.
- Use Power BI dashboards to visualize churn patterns and business insights.
- Explore behavioural changes that may indicate an increased risk of churn.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Programming Language:** Python
- **Data Analysis:** Pandas, NumPy
- **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Machine Learning:** Scikit-learn
- **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI
- **Dataset:** Telecom Customer Churn Dataset

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses a telecommunications customer churn dataset containing customer information, subscribed services, billing details, contract types, tenure, and churn status.

These attributes are analyzed to understand customer behaviour and identify factors associated with customer attrition.

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Load the telecom customer churn dataset.
2. **Data Preprocessing:** Handle missing values, clean data, and prepare features.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Analyze churn distribution and customer behaviour patterns.
4. **Machine Learning:** Train and evaluate classification models for churn prediction.
5. **Power BI Dashboard:** Visualize churn trends, customer segments, and important business metrics.
6. **Insights:** Identify potential churn indicators to support customer retention decisions.

## 📊 Expected Outcomes

- Better understanding of telecom customer churn patterns.
- Identification of customer characteristics associated with churn.
- Predictive insights into potential customer attrition.
- Interactive visualizations for business analysis.
- Data-driven support for customer retention strategies.
